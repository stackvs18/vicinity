    [03] How it works

    Open map data, real streets, one honest number

    01 · Find

    Every place nearby

    Your address becomes coordinates (Nominatim). Every shop, clinic, stop and park within 1.2 km comes from OpenStreetMap (Overpass).

    02 · Walk

    Real walking times

    One routing request (OSRM) measures the walk to the nearest place of each kind along real streets. Not straight lines.

    03 · Score

    Out of 100

    Each category is worth points. Closer and more choice score higher. Every result is saved in PostgreSQL, so repeats are instant.
